  <div class="home-hero__body">
    <p>NTU Sentinels is the student-led cybersecurity society at Nanyang Technological University. Founded and run by students passionate about digital defense, we bring together learners, researchers, and builders who want to understand how modern systems break and how to secure them.</p>

    <p>Our focus is practical and hands on. Members train together for local and international Capture The Flag competitions, breaking down challenges across web security, reverse engineering, binary exploitation, digital forensics, and cryptography. We host regular technical workshops and practice sessions where students learn through direct experimentation on real testbeds.</p>

    <p>Beyond competitions, we work toward a safer digital campus for NTU and Singapore. We organize cybersecurity clinics and awareness drives that help students protect their personal devices, spot social engineering attempts, and adopt safer digital habits. We also connect members with industry practitioners and alumni for talks and guidance.</p>

    <p>Whether you are exploring security concepts for the first time or actively hunting vulnerabilities, Sentinels provides the community, resources, and shared drive to grow your skills.</p>
